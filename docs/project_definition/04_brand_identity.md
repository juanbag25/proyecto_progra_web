# Brand Identity

## Brand Names Ideas

- **NutriCart**
- **MacroBasket**
- **CartFit**
- **FittList** (or **FitList**)
- **Listify Nutrition**

_(For the sake of the documentation, we can refer to the project as "FitList" as a placeholder.)_

## Mission

To make achieving personal nutritional and fitness goals effortless, financially accessible, and seamlessly integrated into weekly routines, permanently removing the psychological burden of daily calorie tracking.

## Vision

A world where optimal, science-backed nutrition is universally attainable and effortlessly maintained through smart, automated consumer choices that respect individual budgets and lifestyles.

## Core Values (The Brand DNA)

1. **Frictionless:** Dieting shouldn't be a second job. The application does the heavy lifting so the user just has to "buy and eat."
2. **Science-Driven:** Absolute trust in the data. Recommendations are backed by expert sports nutrition and physiological science, not fads.
3. **Accessible:** Great health shouldn't be reserved for the wealthy. Respecting the user's budget is a foundational pillar of our service.
4. **Empathetic & Personalized:** We adapt to the user's life (their region, their supermarket, their food tastes, their allergies), expecting the user to adapt their life as little as possible.

## Voice and Tone

- **Expert but Approachable:** Like a highly educated personal trainer who also happens to be your friend. Confident and data-backed, but encouraging and warm.
- **Clear and Direct:** In the grocery store, users need quick, scannable information. Direct, concise communication is prioritized over flowery text.
- **Motivating:** The app celebrates the simple wins (e.g., "Boom! You hit your macros for $45,000 ARS this week. Excellent job.")
