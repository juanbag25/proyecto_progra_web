# App Overview: AI Nutritional Shopping Assistant

## The Vision

An AI-powered application designed to build highly personalized eating routines based on individual body necessities, lifestyle, preferences, and fitness goals. The core innovation of this app is that it eliminates the need for daily calorie tracking by providing a simple, budget-optimized weekly grocery shopping list.

## The Problem it Solves

Tracking calories and macros meal-by-meal is tedious and prone to human error or exhaustion. By shifting the focus to a **weekly shopping list**, users are guaranteed to meet their nutritional targets naturally—if they only eat what they buy, their macros and calories for the week are automatically managed.

## Target Audience

- Individuals wanting to achieve specific fitness goals (gain muscle, lose fat, gain strength).
- People who find daily food tracking exhausting and want a "set and forget" solution.
- Users restricted by a budget but still wanting to meet healthy dietary standards.
- Users with specific food restrictions or preferences.

## Core Value Proposition

1. **Science-Backed Dietary Plans:** Generates precise macros and micros using an expert AI agent loaded with scientific literature.
2. **Budget Optimization:** Maximizes nutritional value within a strict user-defined budget.
3. **Region-Specific Grocery Scraping:** Searches local super-markets (e.g., Carrefour, Coto, Jumbo, Dia in Argentina) in real-time for prices and nutritional data.
4. **Frictionless Compliance:** By simply buying and consuming the weekly list, the user effortlessly hits their nutritional goals without a food diary app.
